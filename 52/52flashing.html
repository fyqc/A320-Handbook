CABIN DOOR PRESSURE LIGHT FLASHING
  • Aircraft configuration prior to reset:

    ✓Aircraft on ground, engines shut down, door disarmed, no risidual cabin pressure on board.


    Circuit breakers to reset:

    ✓Refer to Chapter Circuit Breaker Location list (or MCDU/CFDS).


    Steps to clear warning:

    ✓Try reseting above circuit breaker, if no help T/S by removing the connector from the 14WN pressure switch in FWD E&E. If light extingishes change switch.


    Reset duration:

    ✓5 seconds.


    Results of power up test or reset:

    PASS Fault clears.

    FAIL Fault remains.


    Notes:

    ✓Light normally flashes when both engines are shut down, the associated slide is disarmed and cabin pressure is greater then .036 PSI.


    Sign off:

    TSM52-73-00-810-801.


    Applicable deferrals:

    MEL52-70-02=Cabin Pressure Light On Door.
