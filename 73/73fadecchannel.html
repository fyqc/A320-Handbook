ENGINE 1(2) FADEC A(B) CHANNEL FAULT
  • Aircraft configuration prior to reset:

    ✓Aircraft on ground, engines stopped.


    Circuit breakers to reset:

    LABELPANELNUMBER
    ENG 1 FADEC A / B49VU / 121VUA04 / R41
    ENG 2 FADEC A / B49VU / 121VUA05 / Q40


    Steps to clear warning:

    ✓If this alert triggers at engine start, apply the following procedures:

    1. Aircraft on ground.

    2. Set ENG MASTER SW (affected) to off.

    3. Confirm ENG (FADEC) GND PWR (affected) pb-sw to OFF. Wait appprox 1 minute in order to have N2 below 5%.

    4. Cycle/Reset above affected C/B's.

    5. After ECU power-up sequence, consider a engine dry crank (if applicable).

    6. Restart the engine.


    Reset duration:

    ✓2 seconds.


    Results of power up test or reset:

    PASS Fault Clears

    FAIL Fault remains.
